## Quoting other people's fiction

`story-hacker` and `short-story-hacker` analyse existing books and stories, which means they extract
and store passages from work you do not own.

- **Keep quotations short and always paired with commentary.** Analysis and criticism is the
  fair-dealing/fair-use case; a stored abridgement of a whole novel is not.
- **The per-chapter summaries these skills produce are for your own study.** Do not publish or
  distribute them.
- **`public-domain-cleanup` does not verify public-domain status** — it takes your word for it. Be
  aware that a *translation* or a specific *edition* of a public-domain work can carry its own
  separate copyright.
