# Parcel sorting: a tiny example script

Three scenes, about 40 words. The format is the contract make_narration.py reads: `## Scene N. Title`, then a
`**Narration (N words):**` block, then optional bold-labelled notes such as `**On screen:**` (ignored by the parser).

## Scene 1. A parcel arrives
**Narration (14 words):**
A parcel arrives at the sorting room. Someone has to decide where it goes.

**On screen:** the parcel pops in, a tag says it has no lane yet.

## Scene 2. The scanner
**Narration (15 words):**
A belt carries it past a scanner, which reads the QR label and picks a lane.

**On screen:** the belt, the scanner squashes when it reads.

## Scene 3. The crate
**Narration (12 words):**
Then the parcel hops into its crate, and the round is done.

**On screen:** hop into the green crate, a check mark, a "Sorted" tag.
