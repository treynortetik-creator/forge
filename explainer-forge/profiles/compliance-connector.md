# Review explainer

Helps a committee decide about one tool, connector or vendor without reading a packet.

- **Seven scenes:** why someone wants it (title) · what it does, shown with a sample question · how it connects (the
  round trip) · the permission boundary, including a person who does NOT have access · what moves and what stays ·
  risk tier and controls · the decision, with a strip of sources.
- **More data-flow pictures, fewer words.** One object per system; the same object means the same thing every time.
- **Low risk and read-only:** leave out the open-questions slide, vendor training and retention debates, admin limits
  and audit logs when the organisation already covers them. State facts only.
- **Never in the video:** the review form. Fill it in from the same source documents and attach it beside the file. It
  is a record; it must add no work for reviewers.
- **Source:** the ticket and every link in it, the written brief, and the vendor's public documentation re-read that
  day. Search vendors without naming the organisation.
- **Interactive:** one page that plays, pauses, seeks and changes speed. Wanted on top: click a station for detail, a
  try-a-question panel, F for full screen, speed to 2x with pitch kept, a phone layout of its own. **Build path:
  partial:** the bundled page is the base; `run.md` says which of these the page does not have, and those are yours to
  write from the last approved build.
- **Voice:** no settled default. Audition the two bundled voices and one new one, and ask.
- **Pace:** the number above is a starting point we have not proven for this format; set it by ear.
- **No real people**, including requester, approvers and commenters. The owner posts it.
