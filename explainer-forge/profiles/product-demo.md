# Product demo

Shows a real piece of software doing its job. Approved cut: 188 words per minute while speaking, real app full screen.

- **Build path: complete** (real footage on the stage page, cues from one take).
- **Real product, edge to edge.** No fake browser cards. Zoom to make a number readable, then pull back.
- **Sample data only, with a badge saying so.** Check it yourself; don't ask the owner whether it is fake.
- **Callout rings measured on the frame**, checked one by one; every ring has an out time.
- **Human pacing:** a visible cursor, typing at a person's speed, waits sped up.
- **Say exactly what it does.** Ask how product names are pronounced, and check feature names and job titles.
- **Captions** never sit on a callout: move the callout.
- Capture method: `app-footage`. Rendering: `render-and-caption`. Proof: `explainer-proof`.
