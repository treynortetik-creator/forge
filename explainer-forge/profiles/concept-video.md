# Concept video

Teaches people one idea they have heard of but don't understand. The reference cut is this profile: 4:22, 877 words,
14 scenes.

- **Shape:** what it is in one scene · a real example in plain sight · the two or three words you need · the bigger
  picture · the whole thing working, followed one step at a time (the wow moment, mid-video) · what breaks and what
  catches it · one true story start to finish · where it stands · a close that echoes the opening line.
- **Length follows clarity.** Cut beats, not explanations.
- **Third person.** One coworker can be named if it is the owner and they agreed.
- **One specialist word per scene at most**, on a tag.
- **Post:** teaser only. One line on what the thing is, one image, the file.
- **Gates that matter most:** cold script review by a beginner, and the style frame of the wow scene.
