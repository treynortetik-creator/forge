# Story film

A told story with drawn characters. The hardest profile: the two passes we made were good enough for a teaser, not
better, because motion was thin. **Build path: partial.** Voice, cues, prompts, render and proof are scripted; the
drawings, their timing and the judgment of motion are hand work.

- **Owner approves the script and the storyboard** before anything is drawn, unless they hand the storyboard to a
  reviewer.
- **No jokes** in a story told straight. **No pitch.** A tease is one subtle line.
- **Voice:** one take, a narrator with range; direction tags if the engine has them, read from its docs first. If it is
  a paid engine, propose the cost first.
- **Motion:** drawn poses for actions, a new drawing every 2 to 3 frames; wind-up, action, settle. See lessons 27 to 44
  in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/lessons.md`.
- **Voice before drawing:** the take fixes the timing every pose hangs on.
- **Kit first:** cast sheets, scale chart (characters AND props), screen direction of every action in the shot list.
- **Characters act** their lines instead of narrating them.
- Pace number above is the planned range (160 to 170), not a proven one.
