# Voiced deck

A short deck that plays itself, or that someone presents live.

- **One to three slides, under 10 minutes, three ideas.** Depth goes in a separate document.
- **Audio by default**, inside the same file. Name the file with .html so it opens instead of showing code.
- **Sync to measured word times**; builds fire about 0.3 s early.
- **Player:** play, pause, seek and speed come with the bundled page. F for full screen and 2x are wanted too.
  **Build path: partial:** no slide engine is bundled; `run.md` lists what the page lacks and how to prove a
  click-through deck.
- **No real people** on slides or in speaker notes.
- **Research first** when the topic is current: a web pass and what people said in the last month.
- **Animate it.** A static slide with a paragraph is the thing that gets sent back.
- **Character image** somewhere on it, on-model.
- If the owner presents live, freeze the material an hour before.
- The pace above is a starting point we have not proven for this format; set it by ear.
