Let's walk through it together. First, open the settings panel and find the section marked Sharing. You'll see three choices: just you, your group, or everyone. Each option shows a short note about who can view, edit, or measure your work. Pick the one that fits, then press Save. A small green check appears when the change is stored. If something looks off, you can always undo it later. Quick, quiet, and nothing breaks along the way.
